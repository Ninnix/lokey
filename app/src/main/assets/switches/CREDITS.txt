Cherry MX Red: original per-key ABS-keycap recordings by
Hai Nguyen / Mechvibes. The bundled WAV files are slices of the original
recordings, using the project's press/release timing maps, trimmed only
for leading/trailing silence and attenuated uniformly by 20%.
https://github.com/hainguyents13/mechvibes/tree/main/soundpacks/keyboard
MIT license: see MECHVIBES-LICENSE.txt in this directory.

Gateron Brown: bundled original recording.
Source: WhatPulse mechanical switch sound board, whose credits identify
this recording as sourced from the Mechvibes community.
https://whatpulse.org/tools/mechanical-switch-sound-board
https://whatpulse.org/assets/audio/switches/gateron-brown-down-1.wav
The source provides a single recorded tap, without separate release samples.
